## Plain-language outcome

The existing Academy Owner invites one fictional trainer to the academy's existing initial branch. The intended recipient verifies their email and accepts. They enter a Trainer workspace showing only their own fictional staff name, accepted Trainer role and assigned branch. The Owner can end this Trainer appointment; the next request loses that Trainer access. A person who also has another academy/role keeps those separately authorized capabilities.

This prepares the identity and branch boundary for dated training. It does not pretend Trainer Today, sessions, player rosters or attendance work before I3/I4 exist.

## Narrow policy and proposed design details

| Question | Proposed I2c choice | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Who invites/ends? | Current active Academy Owner only; no Manager delegation | Platform grant alone cannot manage tenant staff. Manager policy stays unresolved. |
| Which role/scope? | Fixed Trainer role, explicit existing initial branch after completed setup | No custom role, whole-academy wildcard or branch management permission. Wider branch creation/transfers are separate later work. |
| Information collected | Fictional staff display name and explicit intended invitation email | No real employment/ID/qualification documents, address, payroll, contracts or contact directory. Recipient email is private to authorized appointment inspection. |
| When access begins | Owner approval plus verified intended recipient's explicit acceptance | Staff/invitation can be pending; signup or provider acceptance alone cannot grant membership. Private staff person/profile, identity, membership and branch assignment stay distinct. |
| Trainer can see | Own synthetic staff name, role and branch only | No player directory/contacts, guardian information, other staff, compensation or Owner tools. Later assigned-session roster projections require I3 policy/design. |
| Invitation | 72-hour, single-use, revocable; original-operation delivery retry/reconciliation | Wrong/unverified account sees no staff details. No parallel duplicate appointment or identity merge by name. |
| Duplicate existing appointment | Review the existing same-recipient Trainer appointment; never silently replace/merge | Suspended/ended membership must not reactivate through acceptance. A later reappointment/restoration needs separately approved policy and actual grant. |
| Ending | Explicit Owner review/reason; end this Trainer appointment, role membership and branch access atomically | Other roles/academies remain unchanged. Preserve profile/invitation/audit history. No delete or hidden restore switch. Future sessions/history consequences are deferred to their own workflow. |
| Delivery and test accounts | Closed exact-recipient staging allowlist | Policy/design approval never sends mail or grants access; each actual recipient test retains separate explicit authorization. |
| Data/release | Synthetic staging only | Real staff data/retention, employment rules and production rollout remain open. |
