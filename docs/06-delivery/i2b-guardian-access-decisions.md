Moamen accepted the narrow synthetic policy for connected design preparation on 25 October 2026; implementation/design approval and each actual recipient grant remain separate.

## What this would let the owner and guardian do

The existing Academy Owner selects one fictional player, names a separately authorized test recipient, confirms a synthetic relationship, and reviews the exact read-only access being offered. The recipient verifies their own email, accepts that appointment and sees only the linked fictional player's display name and branch. The Owner can end that link; the guardian's next server read loses access. Existing player-registration contact email remains unverified contact information and is never automatically converted into an invitation or relationship.

## Recommended narrow choices

| Decision | Recommendation for synthetic staging | Boundary / consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Who may approve a relationship? | Current active Academy Owner of that player only. No Manager delegation in I2b. | Current owner, academy and player checked at preparation, acceptance and every read/revocation. Platform ownership alone is insufficient. |
| What counts as relationship approval? | For this fictional-data pilot only, explicit Owner confirmation naming the player and intended recipient, with an audited synthetic-test basis. | Email verification identifies the recipient only; it is not proof of parenthood. This does **not** settle evidence, legal authority, age or consent standards for real families. No ID/document uploads or real child evidence. |
| Recipient acceptance | Verified intended email plus explicit acceptance of the named player's narrow read-only access. Recommend 72-hour, single-use, revocable invitations, consistent with the accepted initial-owner pattern. | No access merely from signup, knowing a player ID, matching a saved contact address or opening a forwarded link. No access before both approval and recipient acceptance. |
| Scope | Explicit player-level links in one academy; evaluate current membership and link together on every request. One recipient can eventually have separately approved links to multiple players; each link is its own authority. | No sibling auto-linking, whole-branch access, global identity search or inherited access through a family label. Ending one link does not silently remove other separately approved links. |
| Visible fields | Linked player's display name and branch only in this first read-only view. A scoped selector can show only that guardian's active linked players, paginated. | No saved contact email, another guardian's details, health, billing, private staff notes, media or unpublished evaluations. No placeholder schedules, balances or attendance. |
| Guardian versus payer/consent | No payer, payment, consent, export, messaging or media authority is granted by this link. No child login. | Later capabilities require their own explicit rules and projections. No assumption that all guardians share authority. |
| End/revoke | Active Academy Owner may cancel an unaccepted invitation or end an active synthetic link with an audited reason. Deny the next server operation; clear old client projections after denial/context change. | Preserve history; no deletion. An expired/revoked invitation cannot revive access. | Suspension/concurrency | Suspended academy membership or an ended link denies access, including old receipts/replays. | Delivery / actual grants | Only controlled synthetic mailboxes specifically authorized by Moamen at the point of testing. Reuse delivery idempotency, unknown-outcome recovery and redacted audit. | Policy approval is not authorization to send an invitation to an unspecified person or grant new privileged access. No messages are sent by this packet. |
| Data class / retention | Synthetic staging only. Preserve relationship/invitation/audit history; no automatic erasure or retention promise. | D-004/D-015 remain unresolved for real personal data, evidence and legal/consent authority. No production rollout. |

## Meaningful alternatives

1. **Recommended: explicit synthetic Owner approval plus verified-recipient acceptance.** Exercises the actual identity/link/revocation boundaries with fictional people, while deferring real-family evidence rules.
2. **Defer access grants; build a non-authorizing relationship review only.** Records pending synthetic relationship requests but never exposes a guardian view. This avoids an initial access rollout but cannot demonstrate the complete guardian-entry/child-projection journey.

## Design/security packet required after the policy decision

Prepare the connected EN/AR phone/desktop path: choose player → relationship approval and access preview → confirm/send → delivery pending/unknown/failure → intended recipient verification → accept → restricted linked-player view → owner ends link → guardian denied/recovery. Include forwarded/wrong recipient, disputed/unapproved relationship, expired/revoked invitation, lost response, repeat submit, same-recipient multiple links, concurrent acceptance/end, membership suspension and two-academy/other-child/private-field denial.

Bound the guardian selector and link lists (up to25 plus sentinel initially), fetch player projection only when opened, invalidate old identity/academy/link results, and avoid real contact/evidence in telemetry or browser persistence. Reuse the actual shell, native controls, Clerk identity, named attested gateway and current database authority patterns. Do not copy the larger prototype's fixture flags as policy or persistence. Define atomic idempotent approval/accept/end operations, minimal audited fields, cancellation/reconciliation and separate permission checks before implementation.

Security is an independent gate: this proposal and the implementing agent's review cannot supply the independent human approval still outstanding. A synthetic staging demonstration must retain declared limits; no production or real-data rollout follows from approving this policy. The actual implementation, hosted tests, review findings and Owner usability acceptance will be reported separately.
