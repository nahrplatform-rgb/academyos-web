## Read, interruption and recovery states

Review controls sit outside the product shell: linked-player loading/failure/no-link, selected-period loading/failure/empty, detail failure with scoped retry, obsolete Alex response after switching to Taylor, selected withdrawal and link ending while open. Retry is contextual; no permanent “Refresh saved state” control. Production refreshes on opening/focus/reconnect/context change with private no-store data and obsolete-request fencing. Simulation action buttons drive synthetic outcomes; they are not a tested network/auth implementation.

EN/AR translation covers labels, statuses, errors and navigation. Branch timezone stays visible with bidirectional isolation around times. Shared phone Back/focus behavior and desktop list/inspector remain. All private fields clear on denial. Guardian has no attendance write, history, Owner/Trainer controls or unsupported operational links.
