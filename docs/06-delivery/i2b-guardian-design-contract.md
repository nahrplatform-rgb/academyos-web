## Connected journey and reviewed projections

Owner selects one fictional player and an explicit intended recipient → confirms a synthetic-test relationship basis → reviews the player, recipient and exact name/branch permission → commits an appointment/invitation operation → truthful pending/delivery/unknown result → intended recipient authenticates with a verified email → reviews the approved narrow appointment → accepts → sees only that player's name and branch → Owner reviews ending the link with a reason → next Guardian read is denied, with no old child projection retained.

Use the selected compact-blue shell, native controls, EN/AR and actual role-specific content. No macOS window controls. Simulation toolbar alone may switch actor/identity/failure cases; production must derive those facts from current server authority and Clerk, never a checkbox or role selector. A saved player contact does not prefill or approve a guardian appointment. No clickable dead schedule/billing/media tabs in the minimal view.

| Surface | Required useful data | Do not retrieve/display |
|---|---|---|
| Owner preparation/review | Authorized player ID/name/branch; explicitly entered intended email; scoped confirmation and synthetic basis | Global people/email lookup, relationship documents, real data, inferred child identity or a stored-contact→access shortcut |
| Owner appointment list/detail | Up to25+sentinel; minimal player reference, masked recipient on list, intended email only on deliberate authorized inspection, current lifecycle/delivery/expiry/version | Guardian session secrets; unrelated academy records; automatic sibling grouping |
| Unverified/wrong recipient | Generic authentication or unavailable state; recovery destination | Player name/branch, intended mailbox, confirmation that a foreign invite/child exists |
| Verified intended invitation review | Approved selected player name/branch, exact offered permission, expiry/current state | Any other player/contact, payer/consent authority, private notes, documents or broader family records |
| Guardian linked-player selector/profile | Active actor-bound link IDs with minimal names/branches,25+sentinel; selected record read on demand | Unlinked sibling names/counts, another guardian's email, player contact, health, finance, media, unpublished content |
| Owner ending/revoking | Current link/invite state/version, exact selected player/scope, required synthetic reason and confirmation | Delete action, hidden restore toggle or unrelated-link effects |

EN/AR mixed-direction emails/IDs use explicit isolation; forms have required labels, error summary/focus and disabled pending submit. Preview covers empty/unapproved, not verified, wrong account, other child, expired/revoked/ended, unavailable lookup, pending save, failed delivery, uncertain appointment reconciliation and next-read denial. Freshness failures never look like an empty family. Actual screen-reader/physical-device checks remain a separate requirement.

## Proposed storage, operations and lifecycle

Private guardian person/identity binding, guardian memberships, player-level relationship/link, invitation/delivery and immutable audit/operation references remain distinct. No new public direct table grants. Persist explicit academy/player/intended-recipient binding and an auditable synthetic approval basis; do not store legal/identity-document assertions or new consent fields in this pilot. A relationship label is not payer or consent authority.

Proposed life states: prepared/reviewed client draft → invitation pending recipient acceptance → active link → ended; unaccepted invitation may expire or be revoked. Delivery status is independent from relationship authority. Create appointment/invitation, receipt and audit atomically; send through a durable idempotent delivery record after commit. Retry publication/delivery for the original invitation rather than minting new authority. Recipient acceptance atomically rechecks current invitation, approval, intended verified email and current membership restrictions before binding the guardian and activating exactly that player link. Existing suspended memberships must never be reactivated by invitation acceptance.

Owner commands recheck current active Owner, academy and player. Guardian reads recheck current active Guardian membership and that specific active player link, with an explicit guardian field projection even if the identity also has another role. Platform grant alone cannot approve a tenant link or read the child. Pending acceptance rechecks the approving Owner's authority. Ownership transfer is not implemented by this slice; future transfer/recovery policy must separately define its effects rather than assuming this simulation settled them.

Acceptance, cancellation, ending and operation recovery use stable actor/operation IDs, canonical intent fingerprints, expected lifecycle versions, deterministic locks and current authority. Concurrent accept/revoke or end/read must have a defined serial commit boundary; a revocation committed before the authorization boundary denies the next request. An exact replay cannot revive an ended link or suspended membership. Ending one link must not silently erase another separately approved link or history. Restoration requires a fresh approved appointment/acceptance.

The M1 and people receipt stores must not expose guardian invitation/recipient/child data through a broadly authorized old lookup. Inspect current gateway allowlists, subject proof, shared limiter and recovery/cancellation fences; extend only exact named operations after negative regression tests. Do not insert a privileged bypass or use the service key as caller authority. Recipient identity proof is generated server-side from current provider-verified addresses; no body-supplied verified-email flag is trusted.

## Bounded retrieval, freshness and performance/cost proposals

One useful authorized page at entry; lists25+sentinel with stable cursors, no global total, no multi-child/contact fan-out. Detail/permission review loaded on open; one minimal selected-child projection. Private no-store responses, abort/ignore old actor/academy/link work, clear drafts and private projections on real identity/context change. A preview actor switch is a design control, not an auth implementation. Recheck on re-entry/resume and after mutation. No old projection authorizes a read or write. Persist only opaque scoped operation recovery references, never emails/reasons/child data in browser storage or telemetry.

Proposed budgets (unmeasured, not SLA): response≤32KiB; input≤8KiB including proofs supplied internally; DB statement3s/lock2s, app route15s; maintain existing shared60-read/10-write per-minute transport and separate route-entry limits unless a reviewed change is necessary. Target first useful view p95≤2.5s and warm read p95≤800ms/mutation≤1200ms on named device/network profiles; feedback≤100ms and CLS≤0.1. Measure at least50 samples/profile with cold starts, full bytes/rows/plans, provider commands, limiter retries and queued delivery outcomes reported separately. One invitation per deliberate approved operation; no mail on ordinary reads or acceptance retries. Actual recipient authorization, rate/cost caps and worker authority are required before any hosted send.

## Security/recovery acceptance and rollout

Required real cases after design approval: two academies, two players/sibling and explicit other-child lookup; correct and wrong recipient/email; no-link signup; owner/platform-only/wrong-role; active Guardian plus suspended/ended membership; active link versus ended/expired/revoked invitation; current Guardian read projected fields only; context-switch stale response; duplicate/replayed/changed intent; concurrent acceptance/revocation/ending; queued delivery publication failure, delivery unknown and commit-response loss; minimal receipt ACLs; no unauthorized export/media/notification; private-table grants/RLS and signed direct-RPC failure. Never call simulation results database or hosted security evidence.

Contain leaks by disabling new entry capabilities/RPCs and stopping related delivery, while preserving audit/link history. Restore an app build or repair forward; no destructive rollback over later writes. Before a hosted migration: current encrypted backup, restored-baseline rehearsal, exact gateway/app compatibility, security/advisor review and separately authorized synthetic recipients/grants. Independent human security approval is still outstanding and cannot be supplied by this implementing agent. No production/real-family rollout follows from policy or design acceptance.
