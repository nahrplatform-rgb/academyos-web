## Multisport and resource boundary

The source brief mentions football, swimming, racket sports and martial arts, and illustrative pitch, pool, court and mat contexts. Player validation and creation contain swimming level, stroke, `lane_group`, confidence and restrictions, football and racket fields, and martial arts style/rank. Scheduling validation also declares `pool_name`, `lane_group`, `court_number`, `pitch_number` and `mat_number` as free-form session attributes. `lane_group` is evidence of a player attribute, not proof of a first-class pool/lane resource, booking, capacity or collision system. This bounded scan did not establish those resource models. Investigate source and intended outcomes before making a multisport resource plan; do not infer functionality from sport examples.
