# Product outcomes, simplicity and Owner control

## Owner direction — 7 November 2026

AcademyOS exists to make running an academy easier, clearer and more valuable. An Academy Owner should understand the business at any point, finish routine work quickly, retain control and delegate safely. The Platform Owner needs a separate complete workspace for running the AcademyOS business: academies, subscriptions, platform finance, services, support and operating tools. These are two connected customers with different jobs and authority.

The Owner also clarified that PYSA contains defects and unnecessary work, especially in finance. Its complete useful capabilities must be investigated; its implementation and administrative complexity must not become the specification by default. The two [reference conversations](source-conversation-review.md) explain business intent and audit lessons. Latest AcademyOS decisions still govern.

“Better than anything in the market” is the product ambition. It is not an established comparative result. No competitor benchmark, user research, measured time saving or market-leadership claim was produced by this documentation checkpoint. Future comparisons need declared products/versions, representative tasks, evidence and criteria; do not add features solely to match a competitor's list.

## What the Academy Owner must be able to answer

| Question | Product response | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| What is happening today? | Correct sessions, staffing, resources and changes; direct entry to the next task | Branch/sport/timezone context, publication and completeness are visible |
| What needs my attention, and why? | A small actionable list ordered by declared priority, with the exact blocked/missing fact | A failed read is Unavailable, not zero; routine completed work does not become another approval |
| Who is this player, and what matters now? | One useful profile joining permitted identity, participation, relationships and current activity | Purposeful field groups; no duplicate person entry or unnecessary sensitive fields |
| Who will do this work? | Named responsibility, permitted branch/capability and clear handoff | Current server authority, audit and revocation; no shared password or inferred powers |
| Has the work actually finished? | Clear distinction between draft, saved on device, pending, confirmed and refused | Exact original-operation recovery; no false success after a partial write |
| What money is owed or received, and why? | Plain statements and reconciled amounts linked to their source | Separate ledgers, explicit effective rules, immutable corrections and no invented zero |
| What changed while I was away? | Relevant changes applied quietly, with meaningful differences available | Preserve route, scroll, selection and draft; fence actual lost access |
| Can I keep working if the connection changes? | Normal prepared screens and supported Save actions continue | Compatible permitted local data, honest pending state and automatic safe reconciliation |

The Owner need not open every date, inspect every record or approve every routine action to feel in control. Summaries must be reliable and drillable; exceptions must be specific. Existing accepted different-account review, publication and correction rules remain. Removing an accepted approval or adding delegated financial powers requires the relevant decision; this simplicity goal does not silently change authority.

## What the Platform Owner must control

| Business job | Connected target | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Admit and onboard academies | Academy directory → admission/appointment → delivery/acceptance/setup progress → usable academy | Lifecycle metadata is separate from private child/tenant records |
| Offer and administer services | Versioned plans, capabilities, limits and effective academy assignment; understandable enabled/restricted states | Feature readiness, entitlement and person authority are different |
| Collect and reconcile platform revenue | Platform invoice/payment/proof where selected → reconciled subscription state → statement/history | No merging of family money, Trainer compensation or academy merchant accounts |
| Understand business health | Declared acquisition/setup/renewal/service-use and financial measures with drilldown | Definitions and source completeness before charts; new telemetry requires its contract |
| Resolve customer problems | Scoped support, failed delivery/job queues, safe diagnostics, accountable intervention | No implicit impersonation or routine tenant-content access |
| Operate the service economically | Measured request/payload/provider usage, incidents, backups/restore and release history | Estimated cost is labelled; no paid change or promotion authority from a dashboard |

These capabilities belong in `MP-B08` and related service/reporting batches. They are a complete workstream, not an extra menu on the Academy Owner's screen. Candidate analytics such as retention, recurring revenue or service margin need exact definitions and actual data before implementation or claims.

## A value test for every module, field and action

Before adding a field, screen, review or integration, the batch packet records:

1. **User and job:** who needs it, how often, what decision/work it supports and why the current route is insufficient.
2. **Expected value:** time or repeated entry saved, error avoided, clearer decision, useful delegation or business control. Link the applicable stable `VAL` IDs from the [outcome catalog](product-outcomes.csv).
3. **Smallest complete journey:** sensible defaults, progressive detail and one authoritative record. Routine work uses the fewest meaningful steps while preserving accepted consequences and recovery.
4. **Complexity budget:** extra mandatory fields, screens, confirmations, background requests and recurring admin work. Explain each; prefer conditional/optional detail where its purpose permits.
5. **Control and trust:** exact role/tenant/field scope; what is automatic, what is reviewed and why; source/version/history; interruption and offline outcomes.
6. **Proof:** realistic task, baseline where available, expected visible result and measurement method. Unmeasured benefits remain targets, not accomplished savings.

Do not turn this test into another user-facing checklist or a new global approval gate. It is engineering/design preparation inside the existing batch packet. Reuse settled rules. Escalate only genuinely missing decisions that block the dependent behavior, while continuing ready independent work.

## How PYSA capabilities are adopted

| Disposition | Meaning for AcademyOS | Evidence/authority needed |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve the useful outcome | Keep a demonstrated user job, useful edge case or tested invariant | Source/audit purpose plus AcademyOS requirement/action join |
| Adapt | Keep the job with the selected role, sport, tenant, local/sync and Operations contracts | Explicit changed-state/field mapping; no inherited permissions |
| Simplify or replace the mechanism | Reduce duplicate input, ceremony, competing states or unreliable calculations | Show the simpler complete journey and how controls/outcomes survive |
| Omit a legacy implementation detail | Do not reproduce a broken shortcut, unused control or internal structure merely for parity | Record source evidence and reason; retain the corresponding useful requirement if one exists |
| Propose excluding a business capability | Deliberately reduce agreed product scope | Owner scope decision, changed canonical joins and visible future/excluded disposition |
| Investigate | Purpose, actual pipeline or correctness is not established | Name the gap and next source/action/persistence/role check; no invented absence claim |

The current `PY` adoption values are reference recommendations, not a blanket approval to implement or exclude. In the generated capability/field maps, outcome joins identify the value question; they do not prove the field is necessary. A complete inventory is a discovery aid, not a mandate to recreate every old field, setting, menu, approval or formula. Finance follows the [dedicated simplification specification](finance-simplification-and-control.md).

## Measurement and acceptance

Use the same declared actor, data volume, locale, device/container, network and task for before/after comparisons. Capture meaningful task time, repeated entry, consequential mistakes, forced steps, request/byte counts, recovery success and whether the user understood the result. A lower click count cannot compensate for a wrong ledger or hidden failure. Automation success means the intended result was confirmed, not that a loading animation disappeared.

`VAL-01`–`VAL-12` are **additive planning outcomes**, not replacements for AO/AT, journey/action IDs or EX evidence. Their catalog defines candidate measures; baseline/targets are not yet quantified unless a specific receipt already measures them. Existing performance thresholds remain separate requirements. Do not invent aggregate percentages or survey results.

At each coherent batch checkpoint, demonstrate a normal user story, its important exception and return/recovery. Show the changed working experience against the Operations reference, not only a feature inventory. Record unresolved usability, policy, measurement and delivery gaps separately. Owner working acceptance and exact-source hosted/installed proof remain distinct from local checks and product ambition.
