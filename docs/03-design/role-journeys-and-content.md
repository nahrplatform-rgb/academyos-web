# Role journeys, interaction priorities and product language

## Owner: prepare and run an academy

The owner signs in, selects the academy, sees a setup checklist grounded in saved records, configures a branch and sport, invites staff and registers a player with a verified guardian. They create a program, review generated dates and publish a session. The workspace then highlights missing trainer coverage, unresolved attendance, payment exceptions and overdue operational tasks. Every alert leads to the relevant filtered screen, preserving the selected academy and date.

The owner can trace a number back to source records: an outstanding balance leads to bills and allocations; an attendance rate leads to included sessions; a payroll total leads to effective contracts and earning lines. Avoid impressive figures without a visible definition.

## Manager: execute today's operations

The manager starts with their branches, today's sessions, staff coverage and exceptions. They can register or update permitted people, manage rosters and prepare authorized work. A restricted owner approval explains “Owner approval required” and identifies the next step without exposing private amounts the manager cannot access. Their home does not fail because an entitlement helper only recognizes owners.

## Trainer: finish a session on a phone

Opening Today should reveal the next assigned dated session and one primary action: “Open attendance.” The roster supports fast scanning and manual recovery. Every mark shows its true saved/queued/conflict status. Network loss keeps a clear local queue indicator; reconnect does not hide rejected items. After the session, the trainer resolves incomplete marks and, if assigned, drafts feedback. Their compensation page is separate and read-only for approved statements.

Avoid a full management dashboard on the trainer's phone. Prioritize thumb reach, bright outdoor legibility, large targets and limited text entry. Confirm bulk changes and show affected counts. Test one-handed use and interrupted network transitions.

## Guardian: understand a child's next step

The guardian sees a child switcher only for verified active links, the next session with location/time, a relevant academy update and any bill they are authorized to manage. Opening Progress shows published feedback in plain language. Private staff notes and another guardian's data never appear. Payment confirmation gives a receipt and clear settlement status; an interrupted return says verification is pending.

Link, unlink and consent screens explain the specific relationship and purpose. Do not bundle public photo sharing, operational communication and account creation into one vague checkbox. The authority and age policy determine who can consent.

## Player: age-appropriate participation

The player sees their own sessions, attendance and published development goals only if D-004 permits an account. Use encouraging factual language without rankings or public comparisons by default. Money, guardian contact management and private staff notes are absent unless an explicitly approved adult-player policy grants them.

## Platform admin: operate the service

The platform admin starts with tenant health, subscription exceptions, job failures and support. They can identify a failing workflow through operation IDs and redacted diagnostics. Accessing tenant-sensitive data is an exceptional policy-controlled action, not a routine dashboard shortcut. Billing evidence and recovery receipts remain auditable.

## Content rules and example strings

| Situation | Preferred language |
|---|---|
| Saved attendance | “Attendance saved.” / “تم حفظ الحضور.” |
| Local queue | “Saved on this device. Waiting to sync.” / “تم الحفظ على هذا الجهاز. في انتظار المزامنة.” |
| Unknown payment result | “We have not confirmed this payment yet. Check its status before trying again.” |
| Empty search | “No players match these filters.” |
| Failed search | “Players could not be loaded. Try again.” |
| Stale edit | “This session changed while you were editing. Review the latest version.” |
| Access changed | “Your access to this academy has changed.” |
| Partial upload | “3 files uploaded. 1 needs another attempt.” |
| Subscription restriction | “Your current plan does not include this feature.” |

Arabic examples are draft product copy and need native-language review in context. Translate full sentences, plural cases and date phrases. Keep error messages specific enough for recovery without exposing private record existence or technical internals. Error details for support use a safe reference ID.

## Usability acceptance

Run moderated task checks with an owner, manager, trainer and guardian using synthetic accounts. Observe time to first useful action, wrong-academy mistakes, uncertainty about saved state and recovery without assistance. Proposed targets: trainer reaches today's roster within two primary navigation actions; guardian identifies next session without opening a menu; financial operators can explain receipt status from the detail page. Record failures and revise the interaction before calling the design accepted.
