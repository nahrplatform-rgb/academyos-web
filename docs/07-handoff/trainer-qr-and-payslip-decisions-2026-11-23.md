## Approved decisions

### A. Pay periods, fixed amounts and effective changes

Use Monday through Sunday in the academy's time zone for weekly statements, and calendar months for monthly statements. The Owner can choose a default for new contracts. A saved individual contract determines the Trainer's terms and statement schedule. Effective date changes leave past statements as issued. A frequency change starts after the last period under the old frequency closes. The first new period may be shorter as it joins the new calendar cycle.

Allocate fixed pay across calendar days in the original earning period. A monthly rate uses that month's actual day count. A weekly rate uses seven days. A fixed-pay line accrues for the days the terms apply, including weekends. Calculate precise fractions in the smallest accounting unit, then round the cumulative total half up within each original earning period. Each slice equals the difference between consecutive rounded totals. Use the same day allocations for weekly and monthly statements.

**Worked examples**

- A Trainer earns a fixed 3,100.00 per month and joins on 22 December. Ten active days out of 31 give 1,000.00 for December.
- A full-month contract changes from 3,100.00 to 6,200.00 effective 16 December. Fifteen days at 100.00 and sixteen days at 200.00 give 4,700.00 for December.
- The week of 28 December 2026 through 3 January 2027 includes four December days at 3,100.00 ÷ 31 and three January days at 1,550.00 ÷ 31. The statement totals **550.00**. With a monthly statement schedule, those same amounts appear in their respective months.
- At 1,000.00 for a 31-day month, the first seven days allocate 225.81 and the next seven allocate 225.80. Together they give 451.61, which equals the rounded total for 14 days. The full month allocates 1,000.00.
- When monthly statements end on 31 December, the first weekly statement can cover Friday 1 January through Sunday 3 January. The next covers Monday 4 January through Sunday 10 January. Each date and each eligible earning is issued once.
- A session starting at 23:30 on the last day of the month uses that date's terms and statement period, including when it ends the next morning.

An academy that closes payroll on a different day can choose an explicit academy-wide week start. A scheduled-workday proration rule is another option if the academy intends salary to follow working days.

### B. Included work, extras and attendance exceptions

Pay a per-session Trainer once for each verified eligible completed session. A fixed contract accrues across its effective dates. Record attendance exceptions for review and apply a pay deduction only under an approved policy. For hybrid contracts, the Owner identifies which assignments qualify as extras. The register shows actual coverage, and the earnings review shows why each extra qualifies. Ordinary covered sessions defined by the fixed base contribute to attendance and carry no extra fee.

Recommend zero session fee for a cancelled session or confirmed absence. Unverified coverage, shortened sessions, early departure and conflicting records go to review. A session verified as completed under the approved terms earns its stated rate. An unresolved session has no payable line. Any cancellation fee or partial-session rate requires its own stated rule and example. Fixed pay continues during approved leave in this trial. Overtime, special leave deductions and minimum guaranteed session fees remain separate policy choices.

**Worked examples**

- A Trainer covers two sessions at 100.00 each. The verified substitute covers the third at their own 120.00 rate. The earnings are **200.00** and **120.00** respectively.
- A Trainer on 700.00 fixed weekly pay has a missing scan. The week accrues **700.00** and the Owner can resolve the attendance entry separately.
- Under a 700.00 weekly fixed base with extras at 120.00, two included sessions, one completed and verified extra session, and one cancelled extra produce **820.00**. The session list explains all four outcomes.

The academy can define a session minimum, late-cancellation payment or duration-based scale if it wants those terms. Those choices would require revised expected-amount examples.

### C. Trainer QR authority, arrival blocks and verification

For the first online Trainer slice, let the Academy Owner issue, replace and revoke staff QR credentials and operate Trainer scan, manual arrival and coverage verification. Preserve the existing player scan permissions. One active staff credential per Trainer per academy may identify that Trainer to the authorized register while the appointment is active. Recheck credential, academy, branch, appointment and current occurrence at capture and confirmation. Keep the credential opaque and restrict access to its existing authorized presentation or issuance path. Start with Owner-only staff capture and coverage verification. An explicit branch-scoped attendance delegation can be considered independently of financial authority.

Recommend accepting QR arrival when the Owner scans from **30 minutes before an eligible session starts up to its scheduled end**, using server time. Start the block at that session. When two candidates overlap, ask the Owner to select the arrival's session. Offer consecutive upcoming published assignments at the same branch, with at most **15 minutes between sessions** and a **six-hour maximum from first scheduled start through last scheduled end**. Present candidate occurrences on screen. The scan records arrival. A separate Owner confirmation records each completed occurrence. Routine completed occurrences can be selected and confirmed together. An overlap, cancellation, branch change, missing evidence, or changed assignment calls for a scoped decision. A later block requires another arrival. Record a substitution explicitly with the substitute's eligible appointment and actual coverage. Allow reasoned manual capture for missed scans and reasoned corrections against the current record and version.

**Worked examples**

- An arrival at 16:55 can link to 17:00–18:00, 18:00–19:00, and 19:00–20:00 in one branch. The scan creates one arrival and shows three candidate occurrences. Later, the Owner records the original Trainer for the first two and the substitute for the third. The persisting results are one original arrival and three distinct verified session coverage records.
- A session at 20:30 starts 30 minutes after the prior session ends at 20:00. It requires a new arrival. An occurrence whose schedule or assignment changed after the scan requires fresh confirmation. Repeat scans and a lost reply recover the same arrival. Player QR and manual marks continue to use their existing routes and scope checks.

Per-session scanning is the simpler alternative. It requires three scans in the example. The recommended bounded block supports the Owner's one-arrival workflow while keeping each occurrence verifiable.

### D. Approved payslips and private history

Use a private PDF download rendered from each approved statement in English or Arabic with the appropriate text direction. Show the academy and Trainer display names, period, approved statement identifier and date, currency or fictional units, the earnings breakdown and totals at approval. List linked approved adjustments and confirmed payout amounts and dates as of a labeled retrieval time, with the current remaining balance. Display the confirmed payment method as cash or bank and keep bank references and reviewer notes in the Owner ledger. Recheck current scope on every preview and download. Recommend preserving the existing active-appointment rule for Trainer self-service. The Academy Owner retains retrieval of historical statements under the applicable retention policy. Former-Trainer direct access or an authenticated handover process requires a separately defined access and retention decision.

**Worked examples**

- A payslip approved at 340.00 with a confirmed bank payout of 200.00 shows **140.00 outstanding**. A later approved −20.00 adjustment shows **320.00 net approved and 120.00 outstanding**. The statement identifier and original approved amount remain stable. The linked adjustment and payout explain the current balance.
- A Trainer with an active appointment can open and download their own approved statements. A foreign Trainer and a user whose qualifying appointment ended receive the existing access refusal for view and download. The Owner's ledger retains the issued statement without expanding Trainer access.

An approved-only PDF with a separate payment summary is an alternative if the academy wants every copy of the statement to contain identical values.
