## What is already settled

- Family billing, the academy's NAHR subscription, and Trainer compensation have separate ledgers. Each family bill, receipt and allocation belongs to the academy and the authorized payer scope. A Guardian relationship alone grants no financial access. Posted records retain their source and history, and a correction creates linked evidence. [Family finance workflow](../02-workflows/family-finance.md)
- Consequential operations bind a stable original to its reviewed intent. A lost reply is checked against that original. A provider return screen or an authenticated event with an unpaid state supplies no settlement evidence. Statements and complete exports must reconcile to the same source. [Finance controls](../06-delivery/master-plan-v2/finance-simplification-and-control.md)
- The accepted dated-session slice has no automatic fee, credit, refund, or makeup entitlement on move or cancellation. A future billing decision may define those effects explicitly. [Accepted session-change policy](../06-delivery/i3e-session-changes-decisions.md)

## Verified synthetic workflow

Academy Owners use **Family billing** to select a player and verified Guardian, grant statement access, assign an effective fee, review and issue a monthly bill, and record verified bank or cash with explicit allocations. An Owner can post a reasoned credit or unapply part of a posted allocation. A granted Guardian uses **Billing** to open that child’s statement and download all entries from the same checked snapshot. The statement shows due, unapplied and net amounts. The app records a stable owner operation reference before a write. It can check that reference after the transaction response is lost.

The PostgreSQL ledger stores fees with their calculation version and the issued bill with the reviewed coverage, due date and amount. The receipt, allocations, operation result and audit commit together. A credit or allocation correction adds a linked entry. The signed gateway and the current academy, child, relationship and payer grant are checked at the database boundary. The download rereads all pages at the pinned sequence and refuses incomplete or denied reads. The Guardian projection excludes internal reasons, references and staff details.
