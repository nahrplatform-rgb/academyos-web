## Authority and policy dependencies

D-003 beyond M1 must name manager capabilities and correction approvers. D-004/D-015 must establish guardian evidence, child access, consent, payer authority and retention. D-012 must establish allowed attendance values/transitions, capture/correction windows and session-completion rules. D-006 and fee policies govern enrollment/cancellation money effects. D-011 offline eligibility, storage, retention, replay and conflict authority remains unresolved. All of these stay **Policy to confirm**.

The lab deliberately illustrates only name/contact registration, explicit fictional relationship review, a two-date program fixture, one-session enrollment and simple present/absent values. No birth date, player login, payment authority, consent grant, absence fee, credit, grace period or refund is inferred. No real invitation is sent. Demonstrating a verification outcome is not proof that the example evidence or reviewer authority is acceptable. Unlink illustrates loss of future projection; retention and incident handling need separate policy.

## State and device contract

Views have EN/AR text and logical RTL placement. LTR email/IDs/date instants are isolated; native-language review is pending. Desktop reuses compact reference shell/table/forms. Trainer has next-session → complete-roster task flow with touch selects; guardian has child context → next session → confirmed attendance. Phone uses one column and44px controls, not a three-column table compressed onto a phone. These layouts are source only until rendered inspection becomes available.

General loading, failed-read and restricted illustrative states are available in review controls. Domain states include duplicate candidate, relationship pending/verified/unlinked, empty schedule/roster, invalid program, incomplete roster, full capacity, local unsaved marks, committed/unknown/reconciled attendance, correction pending/approved/rejected/stale and no authorized child. Offline capture explicitly unavailable under D-011. QR scanning, import, archived records, substitution, reschedule/cancel, guardian notification delivery and background generation are retained gaps.

Leaving dirty forms or attendance prompts before discard; persona change discards only uncommitted local intent. Saved fictional records persist per tab; draft forms and unsaved marks do not. After an unknown mark save, the stored local result and pending receipt survive refresh; recheck cannot create a second effect. Unknown attendance is specific to a session; it must not be reinterpreted as another session's result.

## Five dimensions and security packet SEC-B2-design/v1

Usability: distinct profile/link/payer/consent; full roster visibility; clear review consequences and current academy/branch. Speed: next task before history, scoped list/detail, independent failed regions; no whole-academy preload proposed. Correctness: current assignment/link/version at every authoritative operation, no full-session completion from a partial page, approved correction agrees with guardian register. Cost: bounded directory25/page, visible calendar window and selected-session roster; history on demand, no polling/provider fan-out. Recovery: original operation check, no blind replacement; pending relationships; stale review denial; preserved history. None is a measured or accepted result.

Security assets: child relationship, player minimal projection, roster/assignment, attendance version, correction authority/reason, operation history. Trust crossings: browser identity→academy/branch capability; player→guardian relation versus payer/consent; dated trainer→roster fields; approval→canonical register; private operator view→guardian projection; local intent→authoritative receipt. No new provider/file/payment/offline boundary is implemented.

Prevention requirements: derive actor/academy on server; check current capabilities, assignment, active relationship and field projections; same-academy FKs/resources; serialize capacity and correction/version; dedupe operation/payload; reject stale/foreign targets; minimize child data. Detection: safe operation IDs and actor/target history without destination, private reason or child data in telemetry. Containment: deny changed membership/assignment/link, remove private projection and pause only scoped unresolved work. Recovery: reconcile original operation, reviewed correction rather than delete/overwrite, preserve financial history and require separate downstream-event reconciliation. Security review is independent of visual approval.

Required actual-layer cases: two-tenant/branch/player substitution; foreign evidence; wrong role/manual vs QR parity; revoked assignment/link while open; capacity race; same-key changed payload; lost response pre/post commit; mixed/partial roster; stale correction approval; after-approval family projection; internal-note exclusion; context switch with delayed response; physical offline remains out of scope. Local model checks cannot close these server/DB/browser/provider/security cases.
