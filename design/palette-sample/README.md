# Palette exploration —29 October2026

Standalone fictional attendance samples. No app, permissions, database or hosted changes. The Owner preferred `interior-colours.html` to the earlier olive and plain monochrome samples, then requested more directions and dark-mode exploration. This is visual preference feedback, not final production-theme acceptance or approval of bulk attendance writes.

Open `theme-explorer.html` to compare four directions, each in light/dark: Charcoal spectrum, Lagoon & slate, Orchid & ink, Clay & espresso. Buttons switch the palette on an identical static screen; Compare all eight provides a contact sheet. Links preserve selected palette/mode in the URL fragment. No network, local storage, account or live save is used. Roster controls are illustrative and the Save button is disabled.

`theme-checks.json` records eight palette/mode smoke checks, comparison count,390px overflow and offline/page-error checks. `theme-contrast.json` records48 normal-text token-pair spot checks against4.5:1. These do not establish complete accessibility, screen-reader, touch-target, real-device, EN/AR or dark-mode production readiness. Existing text sizes and one-screen composition are comparison scaffolding; the complete redesign still needs its own connected review.

This explorer offers alternatives to choose a direction; it does not add four customer-selectable themes to AcademyOS. The current-source whole-app review remains unchanged and distinct from these proposed samples.
